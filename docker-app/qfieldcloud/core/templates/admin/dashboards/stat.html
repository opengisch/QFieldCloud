{% load i18n humanize unfold %} {% comment %} Shows one total as a big number, such as the active users of the period. Below it, optional rows split the total by source, each with a bar. Reuse it for any card that shows a single total. Args: card: the card to render, a dict with: title: the title in the card header hint: the text shown on hover of the info icon next to the title value: the total, shown as the big number subvalue_count: the number in the subline, e.g. "1,747". Defaults to no subline. subvalue_text: the text after it, e.g. "worked on projects (25%)". Defaults to no subline. breakdown: the rows below, each a dict with a `label`, a `count` and the `percent` of the total its bar shows. Defaults to no rows. computed_at: when the stats were computed, shown as "Updated 5 minutes ago" wide: whether the card spans two columns of the dashboard.
